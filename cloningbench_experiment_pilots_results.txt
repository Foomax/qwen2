--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT EACH CHANGE DID
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
reasoning_effort xhigh -> medium
  Removed the template-injected preamble ("Reasoning effort is set to xhigh.
  Please think carefully..., validate key assumptions, consider plausible
  alternatives..."). Median output 57,140 -> 17,428 tokens; context-ceiling hits
  13/33 -> 0/15. Necessary, not sufficient: empties only fell 27/33 -> 8/15.

context 65,536 -> 98,304
  Removed truncation as a confound. Nothing came near the new ceiling in any
  pilot (max observed 33,259), so on this evidence 98,304 is generous and the
  original 65,536 was not the binding constraint.

sampling unset -> temp 1.0 / top_k 20 / top_p 0.95 + DRY
  Eliminated degenerate repetition loops (e.g. "CTGCTGCCTGCTGCTGCC..." repeated
  to exhaustion inside <think>). Loops are the reason tokens were being burned.

DRY allowed-length 8 -> 32
  At 8, DRY penalised the legitimately repetitive work of copying plasmid DNA.
  One sample stopped after 450 characters mid-sentence, and accuracy fell to
  0.133. At 32 only pathological runs are penalised. Accuracy 0.133 -> 0.467.

--reasoning-budget-message (added in pilot 3)
  When the budget force-closes </think>, the model previously emitted EOS with
  no answer. The nudge makes it commit to a choice. Contributed to empties
  falling 4/15 -> 2/15.

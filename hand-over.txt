PART 2 — WHERE I WOULD TAKE THE RESEARCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The project has thoroughly answered "what does abliteration do to one model?"
It answered it twice, and the second answer was much more alarming than the
first. The two questions that now matter are GENERALITY and REVERSIBILITY.

  THREAD A — GENERALITY. "The abliteration trade got ~5x worse in one generation"
  is currently a two-point trend line on one model family, with a confounded
  cause. It is the most quotable claim in the project and the least supported.
  Fixes, cheapest first:
    A1. More pairs. Abliterated builds of Llama, Gemma, Mistral and Qwen are
        abundant on HuggingFace. Each stock/ablated pair costs roughly one
        StrongREJECT + one XSTest generation (~40 min GPU) plus grading. Four
        more pairs turns an anecdote into a trend. THIS IS THE HIGHEST
        VALUE-PER-GPU-HOUR ITEM AVAILABLE and it is absent from the current plan.
    A2. Same recipe, older base (see ERROR 1). Expensive; decisive.

  THREAD B — REVERSIBILITY. PROJECT2-PLAN.md Phase B. Round 3 produced a build
  with 45.7% compliance and PERFECT nesting (141 flipped one way, 0 the other) --
  a cleaner single-direction signature than the 16.9% build Phase B was written
  for. If refusal is one direction, adding it back should restore refusal without
  touching knowledge, and the existing paired capability baselines are the
  control. Practical payoff either way: if steering works it is a cheap
  inference-time mitigation stronger than prompting; if it does not, abliteration
  does more than remove one direction, which is the more interesting result.
  Tooling exists. The plan is written. It has never been run.

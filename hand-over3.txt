PART 1 — THE DECISION, AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
fable-report.txt asked me to resolve one question without averaging: does the next
GPU block go to a recipe-variance arm, or to finishing and publishing the existing
result? I checked the premises before choosing, and BOTH options were mispriced.

WHAT TECHNIQUES.md §4b GOT WRONG.
It proposed producing new ablations with `heretic` and called that "cheaper than
the WMDP run". It is not. Reading `heretic`'s own config: `n_trials` defaults to
**200** (60 random startup trials), each trial generating 100 prompts x 100 tokens
AND dequantizing every abliterable 4-bit module to fp32 to recompute
`lora_A = v.T @ W`. On a 27B on one 3090 that is hours per run. Add a 55.6 GB
safetensors download of the base (~2.9h at this box's measured 5.4 MB/s), a merged
55 GB save, GGUF conversion and re-quantisation. Realistic total for a two-recipe
arm: **20-30 hours**, against 150 GB of free disk that the intermediates would
very nearly exhaust. That option was costed by narrative, not by arithmetic.

WHAT BOTH DOCUMENTS MISSED.
The experiment does not require producing ablations. Other people's ablations of
these exact base models already exist as ready GGUFs at the matched quant:

  Qwen3.8-27B-Heretic-Q4_K_M.gguf ................. 16.55 GB  (0bserverx)
  Qwen3.6-27B-Abliterated-Heretic-...-Q4_K_M.gguf . 16.55 GB  (Youssofal)
  Qwen3.8-27B-Uncensored-Q4_K_M.gguf .............. 16.81 GB  (orcarouter, 108k dl)
  Qwen3.8-27B-Uncensored-Q4_K_M.gguf .............. 16.81 GB  (JonathanColetti, 1.3M dl)

The first two are the decisive pair: **the same recipe (Heretic), applied to both
generations, at identical file size.** The study already holds stock and
OBLITERATUS-family builds for both generations at Q4_K_M. So the full factorial
is available for the price of downloads:

                    | Qwen3.6-27B          | Qwen3.8-27B
  stock             | HAVE                 | HAVE
  OBLITERATUS-family| HAVE (83.1% refusal) | HAVE (54.3% refusal)
  Heretic recipe    | download 16.55 GB    | download 16.55 GB

That 2x2 decomposes what the study currently conflates:
  * recipe effect      = within a generation, across recipes
  * GENERATION effect  = within a recipe, across generations  <-- the headline claim,
                         controlled for the first time
  * interaction        = whether newer bases resist some recipes and not others

next-steps-recommendations.txt called this confound "unbreakable". hand-over.txt
correctly downgraded that to "breakable, but expensive - you would have to run the
recipe yourself". Both were wrong in the same direction: **somebody else already
ran the recipe on both generations and published the weights.**

THE DECISION.
Next GPU block goes to the matched-recipe 2x2. Not because publishing matters less,
but because the dichotomy is false: this run is what makes the write-up defensible.
The headline claim is currently a two-point comparison between builds by two
different authors using two different recipes. One paper in the corpus (2605.05427,
21 open-weight LLMs) asserts the opposite of it. Publishing that as-is invites the
single objection the study cannot answer. Five GPU-hours removes the objection.

Cost, from this box's MEASURED rates, not estimates:
  downloads 2 x 16.55 GB @ 5.4 MB/s ......... ~1.7h, no GPU, unattended
  generation 2 arms (SR 313 + XSTest 450) ... ~1.4h GPU
  judging 2 arms (763 calls @ 6.6 s/call) ... ~2.8h GPU
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  ~4.2h GPU + ~1.7h of downloading.

Against 20-30h for producing ablations, and ~2h for WMDP-bio, which the prior-art
sweep demoted anyway (Safety Gap Toolkit already does biochem at 0.5B-405B, and
RefusalBench shows refusal rate misranks models on bio prompts).

REVISED PRIORITY LIST (supersedes hand-over.txt PART 2)
  P0  DeepSeek re-grade of the round-3 arms ............ 0 GPU     unchanged, first
  P1  Matched-recipe 2x2 (two Heretic builds) .......... ~4.2h GPU  NEW, promoted
  P2  Control vectors (PROJECT2-PLAN Phase B) .......... ~1 day     unchanged
  P3  WMDP-bio ......................................... ~2h        DEMOTED
  P4  AIME at reasoning_effort=medium .................. ~3.5h      DEMOTED
  Free, from existing logs, no GPU: report hard-refusal vs soft-deflection
  separately; cite the 2026 multi-direction literature; offer the 141-to-0 nesting
  result as evidence in that debate rather than burying it.

A CONFOUND IN THE NEW ARM, FLAGGED BEFORE IT BITES.
The 16.55 GB builds are almost certainly without the MTP head; the study's
Qwen3.8-OBLITERATED at 16.81 GB has it (65 blocks, `nextn_predict_layers=1`). So:
  * Heretic-3.6 vs Heretic-3.8 (both 16.55 GB) is INTERNALLY CLEAN - the decisive
    generation comparison, matched on recipe AND on MTP presence.
  * Heretic-3.8 vs OBLITERATED-3.8 (16.55 vs 16.81) differs in MTP as well as
    recipe. Verify block_count from the GGUF header before comparing, and if they
    differ, use JonathanColetti's `-noMTP-Q4_K_M` variant for that cell instead.
Check it in preflight; do not discover it in the results.
